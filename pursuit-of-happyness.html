Review

The Pursuit of Happyness is a celebration of perseverance, fatherhood, and the human spirit. Will Smith’s portrayal of Chris Gardner embodies resilience and hope in the face of impossible odds. The emotional depth between him and his son is heartwarming and deeply inspiring.

“Don’t ever let somebody tell you you can’t do something. You got a dream, you gotta protect it.”

This film isn’t about luck — it’s about never giving up, no matter how hard the world hits you. It’s the kind of story that reminds you that happiness isn’t found; it’s earned through courage and belief.
