Review

Nayakan is Mani Ratnam’s magnum opus — a powerful portrayal of a man molded by society’s injustice. Kamal Haasan as Velu Naicker delivers one of Indian cinema’s finest performances — fierce, emotional, and hauntingly human. Every frame carries weight, emotion, and purpose.

“Neenga Nallavara Kettavara?” “Naala irundha enakku nallavara irukkum... ketta irundha kettavara irukkum.”

The film blurs the line between right and wrong, good and evil, love and loss. With Ilaiyaraaja’s music and Ratnam’s storytelling, Nayakan stands as a timeless masterpiece that speaks to generations.
